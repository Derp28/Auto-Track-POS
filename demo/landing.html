Ring up a sale

Add parts to the cart, apply a line discount, then pay by cash, external card terminal, or split tender. The receipt and stock ledger update with full tax and cost math.

Return or void

Open a sale from Sales to return selected quantities or void the ticket. Cashier actions ask for administrator approval, just like the real app.

Buy and receive stock

Draft a purchase order, place it, then receive part or all of it. Receiving updates weighted-average cost and the movement history.

This is a demonstration, not the production system. The shop server, PostgreSQL database, sessions, backups, LAN deployment, and recovery checks are not part of this page. Everything resets when you reload, and no data leaves the browser. See the repository for the real setup.
